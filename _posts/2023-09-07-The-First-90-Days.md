[The First 90 Days](https://www.amazon.co.uk/First-Days-Updated-Expanded-Strategies/dp/1422188612) is a book I was given by a close friend when they heard I was moving into a new job. It was written by Michael Watkins, and was first published in 2013.

I found this book really useful to get into the right state of mind for starting a new role, and what I should look to tackle in my first 90 days. I took note of a number of the useful passages, and I am sharing them here for others as they may also prove useful.

<!--more-->

---

## Tasks

### Prepare Yourself
* Orient to business
* Identify and connect with stakeholders
* Clarify expectations
* Identify new skills I need that I didn’t previously have
  * What don’t I want to focus on? How can I compensate?

### Accelerate Your Learning
* Avoid coming up with “the answer” too soon
* Questions and hypothesises
  * Who can provide insights?
  * 1-2-1s
  * Divergent and Convergent opinions
  * Are people from top to bottom strategically aligned, challenges, opportunities

## Match Strategy to Situation
* What STARS situations I have inherited? (page 72)
  * Start-up
  * Turnaround
  * Accelerated Growth
  * Realignment
  * Sustaining Success
* Which to learn first
  * Technical
  * Cultural
  * Political
* How to best lead change in the situation
* What are the implications for the team I need to build

### Negotiate Success
* How to build effective relationship with boss
  * How different from past
* Boss’ steer on which STARS situation?
* Boss’ expectations of me
  * Short, medium, long term goals
  * How I will be measured?
* Boss’ style
  * Communication
  * Frequency
  * How much detail
  * What to escalate / issues to consult with
  * How does it differ to mine?
  * How to adapt to it?
  * Speak to others
* What are my strengths
* What are my weakness, I need support on, what projects to learn
* Ask same questions too my team

### Secure Early Wins
* Change behaviours to achieve business goals
  * Encourage
  * Discourage
  * Communicate effectively
* Who are key audiences
  * What messages
  * What modes
* What projects do I need to launch, who will lead?
* What predictable surprises?
  * Skills gaps
  * Retention
  * Political landmines

### Achieve Alignment
* What misalignments? How to confirm or refine?
* What decisions about customers and capabilities do I need to make?
* Coherences of organisations strategic direction?
* Strengths and weaknesses of the structures?
* What are age core processes, are they functioning? Priorities?
* Underutilised resources?
* Skills gaps and what needs strengthening

### Build Your Team
* How to assess team?
* What changes, which are urgent, backups
* Alignment with push (goals, incentives) and pull (shared vision)
* Shrink or expand team?
* What roles?
* How should decisions be made?

### Create Alliances
* What alliances need building, internal and external
* What other agendas are being pushed
  * +ive or -ive effects on my own
  * Who will support
  * Who will oppose
  * Who can I persuade
  * Conflicts
* Long term broad alliances
* How does influence work? 
* Who defers to whom
* What motivations of pivotal people
* Influence strategy
  * Argument framing
  * Incrementalism
  * Sequencing
  * Action forcing events to eliminate inaction
    * Meetings
    * Deadlines
    * Review sessions

### Mange Yourself
* My vulnerabilities. How to compensate
* Control over local environment
* Ease families transition 
* Strengthen advise-counsel network
  * Internal
  * External
  * Where do I need more support?
    * Technical
    * Cultural
    * Political
    * Personal
